MI WHO WE ARE

A trusted logistics partner across India

AOCL India is a logistics and supply chain company offering reliable, efficient, and scalable solutions to businesses across India. We were established in 2019, and our team carries 15+ years of combined industry experience in freight, warehousing, and distribution.

Our goal is simple: become a trusted logistics partner by providing customized solutions that reduce costs, improve delivery timelines, and enhance operational efficiency — for businesses of any size, not just large enterprises.

We've successfully served multiple industrial clients and are actively expanding into advanced warehousing and integrated supply chain solutions.
